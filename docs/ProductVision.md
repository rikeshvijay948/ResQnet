# Product Vision

## Product Name
ResQNet – AI-Powered Emergency & Disaster Response Platform

## Vision
To build a centralized platform that helps citizens, volunteers, NGOs, and government authorities coordinate efficiently during emergencies and disasters, reducing response time and improving resource management.

## Mission
Every second matters. ResQNet enables faster reporting, smarter coordination, and better decision-making during emergencies.

## Target Users
- Citizens
- Volunteers
- NGOs
- Government Authorities

## Core Value
A single platform for emergency reporting, coordination, and response.
